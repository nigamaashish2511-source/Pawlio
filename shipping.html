Shipping Policy

Last updated: August 28, 2026

At Pawlio, we aim to process and ship orders as quickly as possible. Please review the information below before placing your order.

Order Processing

Orders are generally processed within 1–3 business days after successful payment confirmation.

Delivery Time

Estimated delivery times vary depending on the destination and shipping method selected. International orders may generally take approximately 7–20 business days.

Delivery times are estimates and are not guaranteed. Delays may occur because of customs, weather, carrier disruptions, holidays, or other circumstances beyond our control.

Shipping Destinations

We aim to offer shipping to customers in multiple countries. Available destinations and shipping options may vary depending on product availability and logistics.

Tracking

Where tracking is available, tracking information will be provided after the order has been dispatched.

Customs and Import Duties

International orders may be subject to customs duties, import taxes, or other charges imposed by the destination country. Such charges, where applicable, are the responsibility of the customer.

Incorrect Shipping Address

Customers are responsible for providing a complete and accurate shipping address. Pawlio may not be responsible for delays or additional costs resulting from an incorrect or incomplete address.

Lost or Delayed Packages

If your package is significantly delayed or appears to be lost, please contact us so we can work with the relevant shipping or fulfillment provider to investigate the issue.

Contact Us

If you have questions about shipping or delivery, please contact Pawlio through the contact information provided on our website.
