Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: August 28, 2026

Order Cancellation

If you need to cancel an order, please contact us as soon as possible. Cancellation requests can only be accepted before the order has been processed or shipped.

Returns

We accept eligible returns for products that arrive damaged, defective, or significantly different from the product description, subject to our review.

Return Conditions

Products must generally be unused and in their original condition and packaging. Proof of purchase may be required.

Damaged or Incorrect Items

If you receive a damaged or incorrect product, please contact us as soon as possible with your order details and clear photographs showing the issue.

Refunds

Once an eligible return or refund request has been reviewed and approved, the applicable refund will be processed through the original payment method where possible.

Non-Returnable Situations

Refunds may not be available for products damaged through improper use, normal wear and tear, or issues caused after delivery by the customer.

Shipping Costs

Unless otherwise required by applicable law, shipping charges may not be refundable for cancellations or returns that are not caused by an error on our part.

Contact Us

For refund or cancellation requests, please contact Pawlio using the contact information provided on our website and include your order number and details of your request.
